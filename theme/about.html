About us

LectureFlow is a web-based system designed to transform traditional classroom experiences into digital and accessible platforms. The system enables real-time audio broadcasting, lecture recording, automatic transcription, and note generation.

It ensures that students can access lectures live or review them later as structured notes. This project integrates Flask (Python), HTML/CSS/JS (Bootstrap), WebRTC, and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to provide an interactive and user-friendly system. LectureFlow addresses challenges in accessibility, revision, and lecture management for both students and instructors.

Our Vision

In traditional classrooms, students often miss important lecture points due to distractions or limited note-taking abilities. Moreover, remote learning requires reliable tools for broadcasting and recording lectures. LectureFlow addresses these challenges by combining broadcasting, recording, and automated note generation into a single platform.
